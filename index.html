Our Purpose

Second chances.
Untapped talent.

We create job opportunities for people reentering from incarceration. Stable employment reduces recidivism. Our team members are motivated, reliable, and overlooked by most employers.

Our Story

Built by students who saw the gap

Started by two University of Chicago students who saw a gap: qualified workers locked out because of their record, and businesses struggling to hire. We built Fair Rise to connect the two.

Fair Rise is a for profit bakery franchise. We make high protein food and create structured jobs for people who need them. The model works financially and scales.

Why This Matters

The reentry system
is broken.

Over 600,000 people are released from U.S. prisons every year. Within three years, two thirds are rearrested. The number one predictor of whether someone returns to prison? Employment.

75% of formerly incarcerated people are unemployed one year after release
60%+ lower recidivism rate for those with stable employment
27,000+ legal employment restrictions exist across the U.S.

Most employers won't hire people with records. Those who do often offer unstable, low-wage work with irregular schedules, making it nearly impossible to stay on track.

Fair Rise offers structured jobs with consistent schedules, living wages, and skill development. We remove barriers: transportation support, flexible start dates, and no background check requirements beyond what's legally necessary.

When people have real jobs, recidivism drops. Communities are safer. Families stay together. Tax dollars stop funding incarceration and start funding growth.
